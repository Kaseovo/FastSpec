1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

Personal information you disclose to us

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, account credentials, billing information, support messages, and API specification content you create, upload, or manage in FastSpec.

Sensitive Information. We do not intentionally process sensitive personal information unless required by law or explicitly provided with your consent.

Information automatically collected

We automatically collect certain technical and usage information when you visit or use the Services, such as IP address, browser type, operating system, device characteristics, language preferences, referring URLs, timestamps, and activity logs needed for analytics, security, and reliability.

Information from other sources

We may receive limited information from third parties such as social login providers, analytics tools, and payment providers.

2. HOW DO WE PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION?

We process personal information to:

  • Create, authenticate, and manage user accounts.
  • Deliver editor, linting, validation, and collaboration features.
  • Provide support and respond to inquiries.
  • Process subscriptions and payments.
  • Maintain platform security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot issues.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

3. WHEN AND WITH WHOM DO WE SHARE YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION?

We may share information in specific situations, including:

  • Service providers. Infrastructure, analytics, authentication, customer support, and payment processors.
  • Business transfers. During mergers, acquisitions, financing, or sale of assets.
  • Legal obligations. To comply with law, legal process, or enforceable governmental requests.
  • Affiliates. Companies under common ownership or control, subject to confidentiality safeguards.

4. DO WE USE COOKIES AND OTHER TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES?

Yes. We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep sessions active, remember preferences, measure performance, and improve security and product quality.

You can manage cookies through browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect feature availability.

5. HOW DO WE HANDLE YOUR SOCIAL LOGINS?

If you choose social login, we receive profile information permitted by your social provider (for example, name and email). We recommend reviewing the provider's privacy policy to understand their practices.

6. IS YOUR INFORMATION TRANSFERRED INTERNATIONALLY?

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we apply appropriate safeguards for international data transfers.

7. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes in this notice and to meet legal, accounting, or security obligations. When no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

8. DO WE COLLECT INFORMATION FROM MINORS?

We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18. If you believe a minor has provided personal data, contact us at contact@kaseovo.com and we will take appropriate steps.

9. WHAT ARE YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS?

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing, request portability, and withdraw consent where applicable.

10. CONTROLS FOR DO-NOT-TRACK FEATURES

Some browsers offer a Do-Not-Track (DNT) feature. Because there is no uniform DNT standard, we do not currently respond to DNT signals.

11. DO WE MAKE UPDATES TO THIS NOTICE?

Yes. We may update this Privacy Notice from time to time. Changes will be shown by updating the date at the top of this page.
